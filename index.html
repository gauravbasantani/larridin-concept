A number a CFO can defend

A 3-step view for finance leaders, built on Larridin's own rule: AI capacity is not the same as savings.

Gaurav Basantani
gauravbasantani.com
1The problem

AI dashboards show big numbers like 2.5× return. But a CFO has to defend that number to the board, and capacity isn't cash.

2The idea

Show how sure each number is, what it depends on, and what would change it, right next to the number.

3What changes

Finance makes the renewal call with a number it can stand behind, and knows exactly what to say about it.
